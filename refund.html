Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: March 8, 2026

We believe in the quality of our Masterclass, but we also respect our customers' decisions. Please read our refund and cancellation policy carefully.

1. Cancellation Policy

You can cancel your registration at any time before the start of the masterclass. To cancel, please contact us at clickvesta@gmail.com with your registration details.

2. Refund Policy

Due to the digital nature of our content and the low entry price (e.g., ₹9), we generally do not offer refunds once the masterclass has been accessed or attended.

However, if there is a technical issue from our side that prevents you from accessing the masterclass, we will issue a full refund within 7 working days of the reported issue.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund under valid technical circumstances, please email clickvesta@gmail.com with the subject line "Refund Request" along with your payment receipt and a description of the issue.

4. Processing Refunds

Approved refunds will be processed via the original method of payment within 7-10 business days.
